## 01 / THE VISION

**DOLCHATA Education Together** is an education technology initiative designed to help students explore learning pathways, prepare for examinations and make informed academic decisions.

**Website:** [dolchataeducation.com](https://dolchataeducation.com/)

## 02 / PRODUCT AREAS

**01 — EDUCATIONAL PATHWAYS**

Explore universities, faculties, majors, admissions rounds, eligibility requirements and alternative routes such as vocational education and GED.

**02 — ADMISSION INFORMATION**

Organize TCAS and other admissions information, timelines, requirements and status indicators in a structured student experience.

**03 — EXAM PREPARATION**

Chapter assessments, timed practice and exam-style mock tests, with multiple question sets and progress tracking.

**04 — LEARNING INTELLIGENCE**

Planned AI-assisted error analysis, topic-level feedback, adaptive practice and personalized learning recommendations.

**05 — STUDENT DASHBOARD**

Learning progress, exam history, performance trends and academic planning in one place.

## 03 / DESIGN PRINCIPLES

- **Accessible:** Responsive experiences for mobile and desktop
- **Structured:** Clear educational pathways and admission information
- **Actionable:** Turn assessment results into study priorities
- **Student-centered:** Support different goals and alternative learning routes
- **Responsible:** Distinguish verified information from estimates and predictions
